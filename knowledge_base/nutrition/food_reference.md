## Estimating mixed meals

- Break the meal into its components, estimate each portion, and add them up.
- Cooking oil is the most commonly underestimated item: a restaurant or home-cooked dish often contains 1-3 tablespoons (120-360 kcal).
- Visual portion guides: a palm of meat or fish is about 100-120 g; a cupped hand of cooked rice or pasta is about 60-80 g; a thumb of oil or nut butter is about 1 tablespoon.
- When a description is vague, assume a typical adult portion and say that the estimate is approximate.
